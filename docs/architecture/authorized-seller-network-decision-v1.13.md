# Authorized Seller Network v1.13

CornerOps models 32 founder-authorized seller organizations under `founder_attestation`. Authorization is not proof of stock, commercial terms, purchasing readiness, legal affiliation, or complete UAE market coverage. Intermex is reused as the sole catalog-backed seller with its checksum-pinned 190 products; Casinetto is canonicalized once.

Registry, snapshots and policy checksums are immutable. Unresolved official sources remain `catalog_capture_blocked`. Runtime never crawls, contacts sellers, generates quotes, purchases, or mutates CornerMex.
